# Locations globe imagery

The globe uses actual branch coordinates and current branch photos where available. Accra and Freetown use city images until a branch photograph is available. Those city images are decorative location illustrations, not photographs of the church buildings.

The rotating world overview retains its purple dotted appearance and original dimensions. The Earth texture, connected photo cards and image credits appear only after selecting a country.

## Earth

NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation, August 2004 global base map. Converted from the 5400 by 2700 JPEG to a 4096 by 2048 WebP.

- [Source and image collection](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/)
- [Original JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/august/world.200408.3x5400x2700.jpg)
- [NASA reuse guidance](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/faq/)
- Public asset: `https://images.kharis.org/site-images/main/56fb0dba60f8e277/globe/earth-blue-marble.webp`

## City illustrations

Both images were cropped and resized to 720 by 400 WebP. Attribution is available through the Image credits disclosure beneath the globe. Both are used under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

- Accra: Independence Arch, George Appiah. [Original and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Independence_Arch_-_Accra,_Ghana2.jpg). Public asset: `https://images.kharis.org/site-images/main/0c0031656910b735/globe/accra-independence-arch.webp`.
- Freetown: aerial view over the harbour, David Hond. [Original and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Freetown-aerialview.jpg). Public asset: `https://images.kharis.org/site-images/main/f7a0b56e18f54965/globe/freetown-harbour.webp`.

Assets were uploaded with the existing R2 upload helper. Public MIME types and SHA-256 checksums were verified before the application URLs were changed. The image mapping includes all three assets.
