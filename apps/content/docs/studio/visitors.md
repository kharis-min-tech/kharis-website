# Visitors

Visitors are people who used **Plan Your Visit** on the main site or KP2. Each record shows which site they used, the gathering they're coming to, and whether they agreed to be contacted.

<Screenshot name="visitors" alt="Visitors page with a card per visitor showing branch, site, contact consent and email" />

## Visitor cards

Each card shows the visitor's name, then the branch, the site, **May contact** or **Do not contact**, and their email address.

## What the website records

The Plan Your Visit forms write the first lines of the message for you:

```
Site: Main site
Gathering: Sunday Service
May contact: yes
```

The studio reads those lines to show the site and contact preference on the card.

::: warning Respect "Do not contact"
A visitor who answered no to being contacted has still told us they're coming. Welcome them on the day, but don't call, text, or email them.
:::

## Adding or editing a visitor

Press **New** to record someone who told you in person, or click a card to correct a record.

| Field   | Notes                                                      |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name    | Required                                                   |
| Email   |                                                            |
| Phone   |                                                            |
| Branch  | A list of real branches. Branch admins see their own only  |
| Message | Keep the `Site:` and `May contact:` lines if they're there |

## Who can see visitors

Super admins see every visitor. Branch admins see visitors to their branches. Visitors are personal data: only open this page when you need to, and delete records that are no longer needed.
