# Department Videos

The **Department Discovery Quiz** at kharis.org/quiz suggests a ministry department to each person who takes it, and shows a video of that department. This page holds those 24 videos.

<Screenshot name="department-videos" alt="Department Videos page listing departments, each with a Video uploaded or No video badge and a drop zone" />

## The list

Every department is listed with its description and a badge: **Video uploaded** or **No video**. Use **Search 24 departments** to find one.

## Uploading a video

Drop a video file onto the department's drop zone, or click it to choose a file. The video uploads and saves straight away, and you'll see **Saved** with the department's name.

| Format                     | Limit |
| -------------------------- | ----- |
| .mp4, .mov, .m4v, or .webm | 95 MB |

Once a department has a video, press **Replace video** to upload a new one, or **Remove** to take it off. The quiz then shows that department without a video.

::: tip Keep them short
Quiz videos work best at under two minutes. Export at 1080p or 720p so the file stays well under the limit and plays quickly on phones.
:::

Super admins only.
