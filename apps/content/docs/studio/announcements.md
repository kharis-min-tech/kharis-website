# Announcements

Announcements are news and notices. Each workspace has its own list, so an announcement added under **KP2 Website** belongs to KP2 and one under **Mobile App** belongs to the app. Only published announcements go out; drafts stay in the studio.

<Screenshot name="announcements" alt="Announcements page with a card for each announcement, marked Published or Draft" />

## The list

Each card shows the title and whether it's **Published** or a **Draft**. Use the search box to find one by title or body. The count beside the search shows how many there are, or how many match.

## Adding an announcement

Press **New**.

| Field     | Notes                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Title     | Required                                         |
| Body      | The text of the announcement                     |
| Image     | Optional. Drop a file or paste a URL             |
| Published | On by default. Turn it off to keep it as a draft |

Press **Save**.

## Editing and deleting

Click a card, or press **Edit**, to change it. **Delete** removes it after asking. To take an announcement down without losing it, turn **Published** off.

Super admins only.
