# Ask Grace

Grace is the guide in the bottom-right corner of every studio screen. Tell her what you want to change and she'll point you to the right place.

<Screenshot name="grace" alt="The Ask Grace panel open, with a question about editing a branch and Grace's reply linking to Branches" />

## Asking a question

Click the chat button to open Grace. Type your question into the **Where do I…** box and press **Send**, or start with one of the suggestions, which appear until you send your first message:

- **Edit a branch**
- **Visitors**
- **KP2 events**
- **Messages**

Grace answers in a sentence or two, with a link straight to the screen. She knows which page you're on, so "where do I add one of these?" works.

## What Grace can and can't do

**Can do:**

- Tell you which workspace and section something lives in
- Explain the difference between similar screens, such as Sermons and Sermon Library
- Link you to the page

**Cannot do:**

- Save, delete, or upload anything for you
- See or change content
- Answer questions about church teaching or service times. She only helps with the studio.

::: tip Grace is an AI assistant
Grace is an AI navigation helper, not a member of staff. Her answers come from a map of the studio, so if something sounds wrong, check the page she links to or this documentation.
:::
