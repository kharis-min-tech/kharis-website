# Core Concepts

Five ideas explain almost everything in Content Studio. If a screen ever surprises you, one of these is usually the reason.

## Workspaces

A **workspace** is where content belongs. There are four:

| Workspace      | Publishes to                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| General        | Shared content used by every surface |
| Mobile App     | The Kharis app                       |
| Kharis Website | kharis.org                           |
| KP2 Website    | kharis.org/kp2                       |

Open a workspace from the sidebar and its sections appear underneath it. The workspace you are in travels with you as `?workspace=` in the address, so an announcement you add while inside **KP2 Website** is a KP2 announcement. See [Workspaces](/studio/workspaces).

## Sections

A **section** is one kind of content: Events, Announcements, Testimonies, and so on. Some sections appear in more than one workspace. **Events** sits under the Mobile App, Kharis Website, and KP2 Website, and shows each workspace its own list. **Branches** sits under General, Kharis Website, and KP2 Website, and is the same list wherever you open it.

If you open a section that needs a workspace without choosing one, the studio asks you to pick.

## Published and draft

Most content has a **Published** switch. Published items are what the websites and app show. Unpublished items stay in the studio as drafts, so you can prepare something in advance or take it down without losing it.

Events work slightly differently: an event is shown on the sites ticked under **Show on**. Turning **Published** off clears that list, and the card is marked **Hidden**. See [Events](/studio/events).

## Active and inactive

Branches, venues, and service times are never deleted from the studio. They are **deactivated**. An inactive branch disappears from the websites and the app, but its venues, services, photos, events, and messages are kept, and a super admin can reinstate it in one click.

## Roles

Every account has one role: **Super admin**, **Branch admin**, or **No access**. The role decides which workspaces and sections you see, and the database applies the same rule to every save. There is no way to switch role from inside the studio. See [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).

::: tip Saving is always explicit
Nothing in the studio saves as you type. Forms have a **Save** button, and you'll see a short confirmation in the corner when the change has gone through. If you close a panel without saving, the change is dropped.
:::
