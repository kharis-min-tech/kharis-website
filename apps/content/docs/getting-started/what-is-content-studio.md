# What is Content Studio?

Content Studio is where Kharis Church edits what the public sees. The main website at kharis.org, the KP2 site at kharis.org/kp2, and the Kharis mobile app all read from it. Change a service time here and it changes everywhere that service time is shown.

## The problem it solves

Before the studio, a new service time meant asking a developer, a new event meant editing three places, and a message from the website sat in an inbox nobody owned. Branch teams could not update their own campus, and nobody could see at a glance what had come in that week.

> "Let all things be done decently and in order." (1 Corinthians 14:40)

That's the design principle behind Content Studio, and the verse on its sign-in screen.

## What makes it different

**One place, every surface.** Branches, events, announcements, and testimonies are written once and shown wherever they belong. An event can appear on the main website, the KP2 site, and the app at the same time, from a single entry.

**Role-aware by default.** A branch admin sees their branches and nothing else. They cannot open the newsletter list, the sermon records, or another campus's messages, because the screens are not there for them and the database refuses the request even if they try.

**Safe changes.** Branches, venues, and services are deactivated rather than deleted, so nothing is lost by accident. Every delete asks first.

**Built for the people using it.** It works in light and dark mode, on a laptop or a phone, and there is a guide called Grace in the corner of every screen who can tell you where to go.

## How it fits with Kairos

[Kairos](https://docs.kairos.kharis.org) is the church administration system: members, fellowships, departments, attendance, and follow-up. Content Studio is the publishing side: what the public reads on the websites and the app. They share the Kharis look, but they are separate systems with separate accounts.

| Question                                          | Use            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Who attended on Sunday?                           | Kairos         |
| What time is the Sunday service at our campus?    | Content Studio |
| Who is following up with a new believer?          | Kairos         |
| Who sent a message through the website?           | Content Studio |
| Which event should be on the website's home page? | Content Studio |
