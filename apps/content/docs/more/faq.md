# FAQ

## Accounts

### How do I get an account?

Ask a super admin. They'll invite you from [Users](/administration/users), and you'll get an email with a link to choose a password. There's no sign-up page.

### I forgot my password.

Ask a super admin to press **Send password reset** for your account. Use the link in the newest email.

### I signed in and see "Waiting for access".

Your account exists but doesn't have a role yet. Once a super admin has given you one, press **Check again**.

### Can I have more than one role?

No. Each account has one role. A branch admin can be given as many branches as they need.

## Editing

### Do I need to save each section of a branch separately?

No. The whole branch page saves together with **Save branch** at the bottom.

### Why can't I see Announcements or Testimonies?

They're super admin screens. Branch admins see Branches, Events, Messages, and Visitors only. See the [Access Reference](/administration/access-reference).

### I added an event in Kharis Website. Do I need to add it again for KP2?

No. Open the event and tick **KP2 website** under **Show on**. One event can appear on every site.

### What's the difference between Sermons and Sermon Library?

**Sermons** under General are the records shown on the websites. **Sermon Library** is the separate catalogue the app plays from, mirrored from the church's sermon host. See [Sermon Library](/mobile-app/sermon-library).

### Where does the Bible text in the app come from?

YouVersion. The studio only stores the reference, such as **Romans 8:1-11**, and the phone fetches the words. See [Daily Reading](/mobile-app/daily-reading).

## Messages and visitors

### Does Reply send the email from the studio?

No. **Reply** opens your own email app with the reply ready to write, so the conversation stays in your mailbox.

### Can I see who signed up for the newsletter on KP2 only?

Yes. Open **KP2 Website**, then **Newsletter**. Under **General**, you see every site's signups together.

### A visitor said "Do not contact". Can we still welcome them?

Yes, on the day. Don't call, text, or email them.

## Uploads

### How big can my files be?

Images up to 8 MB; videos up to 95 MB. See [Images & Video](/guides/images-and-video) for how to get files under the limit.

### Where are uploads stored?

On Cloudflare, in the church's own storage. The websites and app load them from there.

## Safety

### What happens if I delete something by mistake?

Deleting is permanent, which is why the studio asks first. Where you can, turn **Published** or **Active** off instead of deleting.

### Can a branch admin see another branch's messages?

No. The database only returns messages, visitors, and events for the branches on their account, even if they type another page's address.
