# Daily Reading

Daily Reading sets the Bible passage the Kharis app opens for each day. You choose the reference; the phone fetches the words.

<Screenshot name="daily-reading" alt="Daily reading list with dated entries, each showing its passage" />

## How it works

The studio stores only the reference, for example **Romans 8:1-11**. When someone opens the reading in the app, the phone loads the passage from YouVersion and shows it in the Bible reader. The default translation is NIV.

::: tip Why the verses aren't stored here
Keeping only the reference means there's nothing to copy or proofread, and the app always shows the reader's own translation choice.
:::

## Adding a reading

Press **New** and fill in:

| Field   | Notes                                                                       |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date    | The day the reading appears in the app                                      |
| Title   | Required. A short heading, for example **Life in the Spirit**               |
| Passage | Choose a book, then a chapter. **Verse from** and **Verse to** are optional |
| Body    | A few lines of reflection shown with the reading                            |

Under the passage, the studio confirms what the app will open, for example **Opens Romans 8:1-11 in the Bible reader.** If the reference can't be read, the message tells you what's wrong, and the reading won't save until it's fixed.

Books are grouped into **Old Testament** and **New Testament**. For one-chapter books such as Jude, the chapter is fixed at **Chapter 1**.

## Checking your readings

[Bible](/mobile-app/bible) lists the next eight readings and flags any reference the app won't be able to open. Check it after planning a week ahead.
