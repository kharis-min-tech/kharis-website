# Working the Inbox

A simple routine for keeping [Messages](/studio/messages) clear, so nobody who writes to the church waits long for a reply.

## Every day

1. Open **Messages** and choose the **New** tab.
2. Open each message, oldest first.
3. Reply with **Reply**. Your email app opens with the sender and subject filled in.
4. Press **Mark read**, or **Archive** if there's nothing more to do.

The sidebar count shows how many new messages are left.

## Who answers what

| Message is about        | Who should reply                                      |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| A particular campus     | That branch's admin                                   |
| KP2                     | The KP2 team                                          |
| Prayer or pastoral care | Pass it to a pastor; don't reply with advice yourself |
| Giving or finance       | The finance team                                      |
| Anything else           | The main office                                       |

::: warning Prayer requests and personal details
Messages can contain private things: health, family, money. Don't forward them more widely than needed, and don't copy them into group chats.
:::

## Once a week

- Choose **Read** and archive anything that's been dealt with.
- Check [Visitors](/studio/visitors) for people planning to come on Sunday, so the welcome team knows to expect them.

## Branch admins

You only see messages sent to your branches. If one arrives for another campus by mistake, tell a super admin rather than deleting it.
