# Images & Video

Every picture and video you upload in the studio is stored on Cloudflare and served from there to the websites and the app. This page covers the limits and how to get files under them.

## Limits

| Type   | Formats                    | Largest file |
| ------ | -------------------------- | ------------ |
| Images | JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF | 8 MB         |
| Videos | .mp4, .mov, .m4v, .webm    | 95 MB        |

Files over the limit are refused before they upload, with a message saying so.

## Uploading an image

Wherever you see **Drop an image, or click to browse**:

1. Drag the file onto the box, or click it to choose one.
2. Watch the progress bar. It shows **Uploading** and a percentage.
3. Save the item you're editing.

You can also paste a link into **Or paste an image URL** and press **Use URL**. Use this for images that already live online. To take an image off, press the **×** beside the URL box.

::: tip New branches
On a branch that hasn't been saved yet, the studio holds images until you save. You'll see **Save the branch to create its Cloudflare folder and upload this image.** Press **Save branch** and they upload.
:::

## Uploading a video

Videos work the same way. Once one is uploaded you'll see **Replace video** and **Remove**. Large videos take a while: leave the page open until the progress bar finishes.

## Getting files under the limit

**Photos from a phone** are often 4 to 12 MB. Export them at 2000 pixels on the long side as JPEG or WebP. They'll be well under 1 MB and look the same on screen.

**Videos** should be exported at 1080p or 720p, H.264, as .mp4. A two-minute clip at 1080p is usually 40 to 80 MB.

::: warning Only upload what you have permission to use
Photos of people should be ones they'd be happy to see online. For children, check with the family first. Don't upload images taken from other websites.
:::
