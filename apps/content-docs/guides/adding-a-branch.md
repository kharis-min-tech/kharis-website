# Adding a Branch

This walks through setting up a new campus from start to finish. You need to be a super admin. Branch admins can follow steps 3 to 7 to update a branch they look after.

## 1. Open Branches

From the overview, open **General** and choose **Branches**. Press **New branch**.

## 2. Start from the template

Press **Branch template**. It fills in a starting set of venues, service times, and the usual parking and public transport wording, so you're editing rather than typing from scratch.

## 3. Fill in the details

<Screenshot name="branch-editor" alt="The branch editor with details, pastor, contact, photos, venues and service times" />

At minimum, give the branch:

- A **Name** and **City**. The **Slug** fills itself in from the name and becomes the web address, for example kharis.org/locations/london
- A **Short description** for cards and lists
- The pastor's **Name** and **Role**
- An **Email** and **Phone** people can use

## 4. Set the venue

Under **Venues**, give the venue a name and address. Then search for it, or press **Look up address**, and choose the right result. That sets the pin on the map, which is what puts the venue on the websites' maps and directions.

## 5. Add service times

Under **Services**, add each regular meeting: the **Day**, **Time**, a **Label** such as **Sunday Service**, and the **Venue** it's at.

## 6. Add photos

Add the **Hero image**, the **Pastor photo**, venue photos, and gallery images. See [Images & Video](/guides/images-and-video) for sizes. On a new branch they're held until you save.

## 7. Save

Press **Save branch**. Saving creates the branch's Cloudflare folder and uploads any photos you chose.

## 8. Give someone access

If the campus has its own admin, open [Users](/administration/users), invite them as a **Branch admin**, and tick the new branch.

::: tip Check it on the site
Open the branch's page on kharis.org and read it as a visitor would: service times, address, and the map pin. It's the page most newcomers see before their first Sunday.
:::
