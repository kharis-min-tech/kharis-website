# Overview

The overview is the first thing you see after signing in. It gives you an at-a-glance view of what's happening across the websites and the app, scoped to your role.

<Screenshot name="overview" alt="Content Studio overview showing the greeting, four counts, upcoming events, recent messages, quick actions and the daily verse" />

## The greeting

Today's date, a greeting for the time of day, and your first name, taken from your email address. The badge underneath shows your role: **Super admin** or **Branch admin**. It is display only and gates nothing.

## The counts

Four cards across the top. Each one is a link to the page where you would act on it.

| Card            | Super admin sees                  | Branch admin sees                        |
| --------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Total branches  | Every campus on the websites      | **Your branches**: the ones you can edit |
| Upcoming events | Events in the next 30 days        | The same, for your branches only         |
| Visitors        | Everyone who planned a visit      | Visitors to your branches                |
| New messages    | Unread messages across every site | Unread messages to your branches         |

When there are no new messages, the last card says **Inbox is clear**.

::: tip Weekly events count every week
A weekly service started months ago still counts as upcoming, because the overview works out its next date. That's why Sunday Service is almost always near the top of the list.
:::

## Upcoming events

The next five events in date order, each with a date tile, the time, and the branch or location. Weekly events carry a **Weekly** badge. Click a row to open that event's workspace, or **View all** for the full list.

## Recent messages

The five newest contact form messages, with how long ago they arrived. Unread ones are marked **New**. Click one to open it in [Messages](/studio/messages).

## Quick actions

Shortcuts for the jobs that come up most: **Manage branches**, **Add an event**, **Post an announcement**, **Schedule daily reading**, and **Invite a user**. You only see the ones your role can use, so a branch admin sees the first two.

## Daily verse

A scripture from the King James Version, rotating by day.

## Workspaces

At the bottom, a tile for each workspace you can open, listing its sections. Click the tile to open the workspace, or a section chip to go straight there. See [Workspaces](/studio/workspaces).
