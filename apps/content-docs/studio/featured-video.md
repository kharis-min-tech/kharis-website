# Featured Video

The featured video is the video on a website's home page. It lives under **Kharis Website**, and each website keeps its own.

<Screenshot name="featured-video" alt="Featured video page with two videos, one Published and one Draft" />

## Choosing the video

Press **New** and fill in:

| Field     | Notes                                 |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Title     | Helps you tell them apart in the list |
| Video URL | Required. For example a YouTube link  |
| Published | On by default                         |

Publish the one you want on the home page. You can keep others as drafts, ready for the next season, and swap them over by changing which one is published.

::: tip One at a time
Keep a single published featured video per website. If more than one is published, you can't be sure which the home page will show.
:::

Super admins only.
