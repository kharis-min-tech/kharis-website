# Release Notes

What's changed in Content Studio, newest first.

## November 2026

### Roles

- Every account now has a role: **Super admin**, **Branch admin**, or **No access**. See [Roles & Permissions](/getting-started/roles-permissions).
- Branch admins see only their branches, and the events, messages, and visitors that belong to them.
- New [Users](/administration/users) screen for super admins: invite people, set roles and branches, send password resets, and delete accounts.
- New **Waiting for access** screen for accounts without a role.

### A new look

- Redesigned sign-in screen, with light and dark modes.
- A new overview with counts, quick actions, the next upcoming events, and the latest messages.
- Cards with search and counts on every list.
- Active, Inactive, and All tabs on Branches.

### Uploads

- Photos and videos now upload to Cloudflare, with a progress bar.
- Branch videos and the 24 Department Discovery Quiz videos can be uploaded from the studio.

### Ask Grace

- Grace, the studio guide, answers "where do I…" questions and links you to the right screen. See [Ask Grace](/studio/ask-grace).

## Earlier

- Workspaces for General, Mobile App, Kharis Website, and KP2 Website.
- Events can be shown on any combination of the main site, KP2, and the app.
- Daily Message schedules sermons from the app's sermon catalogue.
