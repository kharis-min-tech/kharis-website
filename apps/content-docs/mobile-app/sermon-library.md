# Sermon Library

Sermon Library searches the messages the Kharis app plays. It's read-only: use it to find a sermon, then schedule it in [Daily Message](/mobile-app/daily-message).

<Screenshot name="sermon-library" alt="Sermon library search with results showing title, speaker, date, scripture and series" />

## Searching

Type at least two letters into **Search**. You can search by title, speaker, or scripture. Each result shows the title, then the speaker, date, scripture, and series.

## Where the library comes from

The library is mirrored into Cloudflare from the church's sermon host, including audio, video, and transcripts. New sermons appear here once they've been mirrored; there's nothing to upload in the studio.

::: warning Not the General sermon list
[Sermons](/studio/sermons) under General are the records for the websites. The app doesn't use them, and the library doesn't show them.
:::

Super admins only.
